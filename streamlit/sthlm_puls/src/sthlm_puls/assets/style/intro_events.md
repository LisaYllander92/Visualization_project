# 🎭 Stockholm Cultural Guide

Discover the best of Stockholm's cultural scene — concerts, theatre, art exhibitions, and more.

This guide helps you find events happening in Stockholm, plan your visit around the weather, 
and make the most of what the city has to offer.

### What you can explore
- 📅 **This week** — see what's on and find the best day to go out
- 🌦️ **Weather & events** — plan smarter with a 7-day cultural forecast
- 🎵 **Browse by genre** — filter events by music, theatre, sports, and more

*Data sourced from Ticketmaster, VisitStockholm, Fasching and Berns.*
